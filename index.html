About

I’m a Speech-Language Pathologist who believes communication grows best through connection, play and consistency. After a Bachelor’s in Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology and a Master’s in Speech and Language Pathology, I began working with children and families in Doha, Qatar.

My work centers on individualized, child-centered intervention for children with developmental disabilities and communication needs. I assess, set goals, and use approaches such as AAC, Natural Environment Teaching and play-based therapy to build functional communication, independence and positive behavior. I work closely with caregivers, teachers and multidisciplinary teams, because progress lasts when everyone around the child is on the same page.
